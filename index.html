I am a Computer Science and Mathematics graduate from the University of Toronto. I have always been interested in understanding the how and why behind things—whether that's going the extra step to understand a small detail in a math problem, testing unexpected edge cases to make my algorithms more robust, or digging deeper to find a simpler way to solve a problem.

During my 16-month internship, I had the opportunity to work on computer vision and advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), where I developed and optimized C/C++ software for automotive applications. This experience taught me how to take ideas from theory and turn them into reliable, efficient systems through research, debugging, optimization, and testing, while also giving me the opportunity to work on problems where small details can have a meaningful impact.

Outside of software development, mathematics remains a major part of what I do. I teach mathematics and create educational content, and I enjoy exploring mathematical ideas simply because I want to understand them more deeply.
