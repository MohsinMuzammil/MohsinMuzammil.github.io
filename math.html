Mathematics

Mathematics has been a major part of my education and continues to be something I explore through teaching, problem solving, and creating educational content. I enjoy it not just for the problems themselves, but for the process of understanding why things work and finding different ways to arrive at the same result.

Teaching Mathematics

I have been teaching mathematics since 2020, both as a teaching assistant at the University of Toronto and as a private mathematics instructor. Today, I continue to teach through private instruction and educational content on YouTube.

My approach focuses on understanding the why and how behind a concept rather than memorizing formulas and procedures. I enjoy breaking down difficult ideas, finding different ways to explain them, and helping students develop a deeper understanding of mathematics.

Introduction to Combinatorics

A comprehensive exploration of combinatorial mathematics, covering the pigeonhole principle, induction, counting techniques, combinatorial proofs, partitions, permutations, inclusion-exclusion, generating functions, and graph theory. The portfolio includes fully worked problems and derivations involving topics such as Stirling numbers, Bell numbers, Catalan numbers, derangements, Fibonacci sequences, spanning trees, and other combinatorial structures, along with a reflection on my development as a mathematician.
