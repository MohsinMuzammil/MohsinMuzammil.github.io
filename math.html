Mathematics

Mathematics has been a major part of my education and continues to be something I explore through teaching, problem solving, and educational content. I enjoy mathematics not just for the problems themselves, but for the process of understanding why things work and finding different ways to arrive at the same result.

When I was young, I would look into upper-grade classrooms and wonder what all the cool symbols and diagrams on the board meant. I wanted to understand them, and that curiosity has stayed with me ever since.

Teaching Mathematics

I have been teaching mathematics since 2020. My teaching experience has included working as a teaching assistant at the University of Toronto as well as privately tutoring students one-on-one. Today, I continue to teach mathematics through private tutoring and educational content on YouTube.

My approach is focused on understanding the why and how behind a concept rather than simply memorizing formulas and procedures. I enjoy breaking down difficult ideas, finding different ways to explain them, and helping students develop a deeper understanding of mathematics.

Whether I am working through a difficult problem with a student or creating a video, I try to make the reasoning behind each step intuitive rather than simply presenting a solution.
