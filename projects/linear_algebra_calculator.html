Linear Algebra Calculator

A Python calculator for determinants and row reduction, built with two friends at the PyJaC Rebooted Hackathon.

January 2022 Python · PyJaC Rebooted Hackathon · Linear Algebra

Overview

I participated in the PyJaC Rebooted Hackathon with two friends. All three of us share an interest in mathematics and find linear algebra genuinely fascinating, so we decided to challenge ourselves to implement something we'd learned in class as a computer algorithm.

Our idea was to build a Linear Algebra Calculator in Python with two features:

  • Compute the determinant of a square matrix
  • Row reduce a rectangular matrix

Optimization

We tried to optimize our algorithms where we could. For instance, the determinant is typically computed via cofactor expansion, which quickly becomes expensive as matrices grow.

Our approach was to search for the row or column containing the most zeros before starting the expansion. Choosing a zero-heavy row means most of the cofactors are multiplied by zero, which lets us skip a significant portion of the work. It's a small algorithmic insight, but it makes a real difference on sparse matrices.

Reflections

This was a cool experience because it gave me an opportunity to directly apply both my math and computer science knowledge to achieve a goal. Turning a concept from a linear algebra lecture into a working algorithm forced me to think about matrices not just as mathematical objects but as data structures — and to consider what makes one computation more efficient than another.

Working with two friends also made it memorable. We had to coordinate on design decisions, divide the work, and debug each other's code under time pressure — a very different experience from working solo. I also recorded and edited a demo video for the judges, which gave me a chance to practice presenting technical work to a non-technical audience.
